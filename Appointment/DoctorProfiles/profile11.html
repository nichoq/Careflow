Frequently Asked Questions

What types of neurological conditions can you discuss online?

I can provide initial consultations and follow-ups for conditions like migraines, tremors, and Parkinson's disease online.

Is an online consultation sufficient for diagnosing complex neurological issues?

While online consultations can be helpful, some complex cases may require in-person neurological examinations and tests.

What information should I have ready for my online neurology appointment?

Please have a detailed account of your symptoms, their onset, duration, and any previous neurological assessments or imaging results.
