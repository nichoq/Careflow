Frequently Asked Questions

What types of gastrointestinal issues can be discussed online?

I can address questions about common digestive symptoms, provide dietary advice, and discuss preliminary concerns through online consultations.

Are online consultations sufficient for diagnosing all digestive problems?

While helpful for initial assessment, many gastrointestinal conditions require in-person examinations and diagnostic procedures.

How should I prepare for my online gastroenterology consultation?

Please have a detailed description of your symptoms, their onset, duration, and any relevant medical history available.
