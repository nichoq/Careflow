Frequently Asked Questions

What types of medical concerns can you address both online and onsite?

I can handle a wide range of general health concerns, from common illnesses to routine check-ups, through both consultation methods.

When should I choose an online consultation versus an onsite visit?

Online consultations are convenient for follow-ups and minor issues, while onsite visits are necessary for physical examinations and urgent conditions.

What should I have ready for my consultation, regardless of the format?

Please have a list of your current medications, allergies, and any relevant medical history available.
