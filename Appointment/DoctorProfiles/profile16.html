Frequently Asked Questions

What pediatric health concerns are suitable for online consultation?

I can address questions about common childhood illnesses, growth monitoring, and general pediatric advice through online consultations.

Is a physical examination always necessary for pediatric concerns?

While online consultations are helpful, certain conditions, like vaccinations or detailed physical assessments, require an in-person visit.

How should I prepare for my child's online consultation?

Please have your child's health records, a list of current medications, and a clear description of your concerns ready for our discussion.
