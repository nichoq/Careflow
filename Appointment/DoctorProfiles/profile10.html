Frequently Asked Questions

What types of pediatric conditions do you handle online?

I can address common childhood concerns such as fever, coughs, colds, and provide guidance on minor injuries online.

When should my child be seen for an in-person consultation?

Severe symptoms, difficulty breathing, or injuries requiring physical examination necessitate an in-person visit.

What information should I provide during an online consultation for my child?

Please be ready to share your child's symptoms, temperature, activity level, and any relevant medical history.
