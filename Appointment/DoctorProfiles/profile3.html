Frequently Asked Questions

What skin conditions do you treat online?

I can address many common skin concerns online, including acne, eczema, and psoriasis for initial assessment and management.

How does an online dermatology consultation work?

You'll have a virtual appointment where we discuss your concerns, and you may be asked to show the affected areas via webcam.

Are online prescriptions possible?

Yes, if clinically appropriate, I can provide prescriptions during your online consultation.
