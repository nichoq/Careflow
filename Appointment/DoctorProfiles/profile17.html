Frequently Asked Questions

What neurological conditions can you discuss online?

I can provide initial consultations and follow-ups for conditions like headaches, dizziness, and memory issues via online consultations.

Is an in-person examination always required for neurological problems?

While online consultations are valuable, a physical neurological exam is often necessary for a comprehensive diagnosis and treatment plan.

How should I prepare for my online neurology consultation?

Please have a detailed account of your symptoms, their triggers, duration, and any relevant medical reports ready for our discussion.
